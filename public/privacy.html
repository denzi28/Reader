Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Leafpage is a document reader that runs in your web browser. This policy explains what happens to your documents and information when you use it. In short: your documents and notes stay on your device, and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. We do not run servers that store them, and we never see them.

Your documents and notes

PDFs you open, the ink and highlights you add, and the page you were reading are saved in your browser's storage on your device. They are not sent to us.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Sync with Google Drive, you sign in with Google and allow Leafpage to use the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. With it, the app:

  • stores copies of your documents, your ink and highlights, your reading position and the document covers in a hidden folder that belongs to Leafpage inside your own Google Drive, so they appear on your other devices;
  • reads and updates only the files in that folder. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive;
  • reads the name, email address and profile photo of the connected Google account (from Google Drive), only to show you which account is connected.

Your documents and notes travel directly between your browser and Google. They do not pass through, and are not stored on, any server of ours.

Staying signed in

So that you sign in once and are not asked for permission again, our sign-in service keeps the long-lived access key Google issues when you allow access (a "refresh token"):

  • in an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie in your browser, for this device; and
  • encrypted, in a small database (hosted by Supabase in the EU), under an identifier derived from your Google account that cannot be turned back into your email or name.

The key can only be used together with our app's own secret, and only to reach Leafpage's folder in your Drive. Signing out ends the session on that device; to withdraw access completely, remove Leafpage in your Google account (see below). The stored key is deleted the next time it is found to no longer work.

Leafpage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone, and no person reads it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • No accounts on our side; no profile of you is built.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may process technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy policy.

Removing your data

  • A document: delete it from the shelf. This removes it, with its notes, from this device and, when sync is on, from Leafpage's folder in your Drive.
  • Sign out: choose Sign out in the app. The account's documents are removed from that device and stay in your Drive.
  • Revoke access: remove Leafpage at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Its stored access key then stops working and is deleted.
  • Delete everything in Drive: in Google Drive, open Settings → Manage apps, find Leafpage and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Everything on a device: clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Children

Leafpage is not directed at children under 13.
